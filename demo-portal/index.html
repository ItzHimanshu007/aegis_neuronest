Aegis Demo Portal

DEMO — SYNTHETIC DATA

These pages exist to exercise the Aegis browser vision agent during development and judging. Every field is fake. Nothing here identifies a real person.

  • KYC Verification — a synthetic KYC form with an Aadhaar field, a PAN field, a password, a profile photo placeholder and a specimen ID card.
    Same form, with a cookie banner covering Submit
  • Calibration — coloured squares at known positions, for the screenshot/coordinate alignment test.
  • Shadow DOM — form fields inside open and closed shadow roots.
  • Frames — a same-origin and a cross-origin iframe (run pnpm portal:alt for the cross-origin one, on port 5175).
  • Dynamic — a modal that opens after 1s, a form that re-renders with new ids/classes, and three identical "Add" buttons.
  • Hidden elements — every visibility-hiding technique, plus a button covered by a banner.
